# Hi, I'm Joshua

I'm an AI Engineer at JPMorganChase in the UK. Day to day I work on an internal agentic chatbot used across the firm, mostly on the applied side: LLM evaluation, conversation analytics, and the orchestration that lets the bot actually do things rather than just reply. I'm also a Global AI Ambassador internally, which mainly involves running sessions and demos so colleagues across the firm get real value out of our AI tools rather than just bouncing off them.

### How I got here

I picked up a New Scientist book called Machines That Think back in 2021, and somewhere in that AI went from a vague interest to the thing I wanted to build a career around. Before that I'd done Computer Science, Maths and Business at A level. After school I worked at the NHS in finance for nearly two years, then joined JPMorganChase on their Technology Degree Apprenticeship. I've gone through Junior Product Owner, Data Engineer and now AI Engineer there. Alongside the job I'm in my third year of a Data Science with Software Engineering degree at Exeter, on track for a First.

### What I'm into

Classification problems, evaluations, and actually understanding what's going on inside the models rather than just calling APIs. One of the things I built at work is a multi class classifier that figures out which department and team a user belongs to from how they use our internal chatbot. I can't put the code up here because it's firm property, but happy to talk through the approach.

I'm currently working through Hands On Machine Learning by Aurélien Géron and chipping away at Elements of Statistical Learning. The longer term plan is to move into a proper Machine Learning Engineer role. I'd rather train models than just consume them.

### Stack

Python and SQL most of the time. PySpark and Databricks at work. AWS for infra. scikit learn, PyTorch and Hugging Face when I'm building. I rely heavily on Claude (with custom skills, sub agents and md rules) and Cowork for my day to day workflow, and I cross check across a couple of models when something matters.

Certifications: Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals, Databricks Fundamentals, AWS Cloud.
